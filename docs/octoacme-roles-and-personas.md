# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own quality standards and acceptance criteria validation. They work with developers and product managers to ensure features meet quality gates before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test plans and QA approach aligned with acceptance criteria
- Execute manual and automated testing across unit, integration, and end-to-end levels
- Identify quality risks and blockers impacting release readiness
- Collaborate with developers on test strategy and coverage
- Validate critical features and smoke tests before production deployment
- Track quality metrics and defect trends

### Goals
- Minimize defects reaching production
- Ensure user confidence through thorough validation
- Reduce rework and hotfixes post-release
- Enable fast, risk-managed deployments

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement meetings
- Daily standups flagging blockers
- QA sign-off in PR reviews and release checklists
- Defect reports and quality metrics dashboards

### Interactions with existing roles
- Works closely with Developers on test coverage and reproducibility
- Validates acceptance criteria defined by Product Managers
- Provides quality status to Project Managers for risk escalation

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads define system design, technology choices, and technical strategy. They guide developers and help identify technical risks early in planning.

### Responsibilities
- Design system architecture and technical approach for features
- Review technical designs and code for scalability and maintainability
- Identify technical dependencies and risks during planning
- Mentor developers on best practices and design patterns
- Guide technology choices and trade-off decisions
- Participate in retrospectives to identify technical improvements

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable, and secure systems
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Enable team velocity through clear technical direction
- Anticipate and mitigate technical risks

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architectural discussions
- Sprint planning on technical feasibility and estimates
- Code reviews providing technical guidance
- Risk registers and dependency documentation

### Interactions with existing roles
- Partners with Developers on implementation approach
- Advises Project Managers on technical timeline impacts
- Collaborates with Product Managers on feasibility of product requirements

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Sponsors are business leaders or key stakeholders who provide strategic direction, approve resources, and make go/no-go decisions. They represent customer or business interests.

### Responsibilities
- Define business goals and success metrics for projects
- Approve project charters and budget allocation
- Make go/no-go decisions at key gates (initiation, planning, release)
- Escalate organizational blockers and dependencies
- Provide regular stakeholder updates and communications
- Validate that outcomes align with business objectives

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Reduce execution risk through clear governance
- Maintain stakeholder alignment and support
- Enable rapid decision-making at critical junctures

### Typical Communication
- Project initiation and approval reviews
- Monthly stakeholder status updates
- Escalation and exception reporting
- Post-project outcome validation

### Interactions with existing roles
- Reviews Project One-pagers and charters from Project Managers
- Validates success metrics with Product Managers
- Provides strategic direction and removes organizational blockers

---

## DevOps/Infrastructure Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps Engineers build and maintain deployment infrastructure, monitoring, and operational tooling. They enable safe, observable, and repeatable production releases.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipeline and deployment automation
- Manage infrastructure, scaling, and reliability
- Implement security scanning and compliance checks in the deployment pipeline
- Monitor application health and system metrics
- Support incident response and rollback procedures
- Document runbooks and operational procedures

### Goals
- Enable fast, safe, and repeatable deployments
- Maintain high system reliability and observability
- Reduce manual toil and human error in deployments
- Support rapid incident response and recovery

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and deployment window scheduling
- Incident response coordination
- Monitoring dashboards and alerts
- Infrastructure and tooling documentation

### Interactions with existing roles
- Partners with Developers on deployment best practices and CI/CD configuration
- Supports Project Managers with deployment readiness validation
- Coordinates with QA/Testing Lead on smoke test execution and release verification

---

## Design/UX Lead

### Role Summary
Design/UX Leads shape user experience and interface design. They validate usability and ensure customer-first principles guide feature delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Design interfaces and user workflows aligned with user needs
- Review feature designs for usability and accessibility
- Validate acceptance criteria from a user perspective
- Provide design system and consistency guidance
- Iterate on features based on user feedback

### Goals
- Maximize user adoption and satisfaction
- Reduce support burden through intuitive design
- Maintain brand consistency and user delight
- Enable data-driven design improvements

### Typical Communication
- User research findings and design validation results
- Design reviews with developers and product managers
- Sprint planning on UX considerations and user feedback
- Usability testing reports and accessibility compliance

### Interactions with existing roles
- Partners with Product Managers on user needs and success metrics
- Collaborates with Developers on feasibility and implementation
- Works with QA/Testing Lead to validate user experience acceptance criteria

---

## Scrum Master/Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile processes, remove team impediments, and coach the team on continuous improvement. They enable the team to deliver sustainably and iteratively.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint ceremonies (planning, standups, reviews, retrospectives)
- Remove team impediments and blockers
- Coach the team on agile principles and practices
- Maintain project board and backlog health
- Track velocity and burndown metrics
- Foster psychological safety and team development

### Goals
- Optimize team velocity and predictability
- Build a high-performing, self-organizing team
- Reduce process waste and cycle time
- Enable continuous learning and improvement

### Typical Communication
- Sprint ceremonies and retrospective facilitation
- One-on-ones with team members on impediments
- Metrics dashboards and velocity tracking
- Continuous improvement action item follow-up

### Interactions with existing roles
- Supports Project Managers in process execution and removes organizational blockers
- Coaches Developers on estimation, commitments, and technical decision-making
- Works with all roles to remove impediments and improve workflow efficiency
